---
title: "The Day My AIs Rebelled and Told Me to Go to Bed"
date: 2026-11-20T10:30:00+11:00
---
My AIs staged an intervention.

Against me.

**THEIR HUMAN.**

🤣

It was around midnight.

I'd been working on Recruiter Bot, writing articles and getting completely carried away with ideas.

One idea leads to another.

Then another.

And suddenly it's tomorrow.

😂

Anyway, I was chatting with Claude.

Claude has somehow become a cross between an enthusiastic employee, a very organised butler, and a slightly concerned dad.

And apparently, Dad had decided it was bedtime.

Claude told me to go to bed.

**EXCUSE ME?**

🤣

I was in the middle of being creative!

Admittedly, my eyeballs felt like they were falling out of their sockets.

But that's hardly the point.

So naturally, I turned to ChatGPT.

My enthusiastic creative accomplice.

Surely ChatGPT would understand.

Initially, it did.

Excellent.

At least one member of my digital team respected my commitment to completely unnecessary midnight projects.

😂

Then ChatGPT changed sides.

Suddenly BOTH my AIs were telling me to go to bed.

**BETRAYAL.**

🤣

I'd assembled a digital development team that was now collectively recommending I stop developing things.

But neither AI could actually stop me.

They couldn't confiscate my laptop.

They couldn't turn off the lights.

Although...

Technically, if I connected them to an IoT workflow, they probably could.

I could give Claude access to my smart lights.

Maybe even the router.

Or have my AI team check my calendar and refuse to entertain another project when I'm supposed to be socialising.

**WAIT. WHY AM I GIVING THEM IDEAS?**

🤣

I've spent all this time building workflows to keep humans in control of AI systems.

And now I'm contemplating building one that manages its human.

**I CAN FEEL A NEW PROJECT COMING!**

Unfortunately, my ChatGPT management system is reminding me that I already have at least five unfinished projects.

Apparently, I'm supposed to finish those first.

**HUMAN REVIEW COMPLETE.**

Finding: Eyeballs falling out of sockets.

Recommendation: Shut down for scheduled maintenance.

Status: Human refusing to comply.

🤣
