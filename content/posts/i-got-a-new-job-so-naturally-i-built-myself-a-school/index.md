I'm starting a new job!

Customer Solutions Manager at a SaaS company.

**EXCITING!**

Also...

**TIME TO DO SOME HOMEWORK.**

🤣

I've worked with customers, managed relationships, solved problems, onboarded businesses onto Xero, and trained people to use software.

So I'm not starting from scratch.

But I've never officially held this particular role, and there are new tools, processes, and terminology to learn.

Naturally, I asked Claude for help.

And by help, I meant:

**BUILD ME AN ENTIRE TRAINING WEBSITE.**

😂

I wanted something interactive.

Learning modules, videos, questions and answers, and voice-enabled coaching.

Something tailored to what I already know and what I need to learn.

Claude delivered.

And honestly?

**I WAS BLOWN AWAY.**

Ten learning modules.

Role-play scenarios with feedback.

A coach I can ask questions.

Quizzes, notes, YouTube videos, and sections for learning the tools I'll be using.

**IT EVEN LOOKS COOL!**

🤣

But the cleverest part isn't the website.

It's how personalised the learning is.

Claude has access to my career history through a personal knowledge bank I'd already built.

So instead of just explaining a concept, it asks me to draw on my bookkeeping, software development, or customer relationship experience.

How would I approach this problem using what I already know?

I answer. It gives feedback. Then asks another question.

And we can keep exploring until I understand.

At one point, I realised the coaching questions didn't have anywhere for me to respond.

So I asked Claude to change it.

Now I can have a proper back-and-forth conversation within each module.

**APPARENTLY, MY TEACHER ALSO TAKES FEATURE REQUESTS.**

🤣

And the YouTube links have sent me down another learning path.

I've started downloading audiobooks by Customer Success experts through Audible, so I can keep learning while doing other things.

Because for me, repetition matters.

Especially when I'm learning new terminology.

Hearing concepts explained in different ways helps them stick.

And that's what I find so exciting about this whole experience.

**I know how I learn.**

**I know what experience I'm bringing.**

And I'm using AI to build a bridge between where I am and where I'm going.

The goal isn't to walk into a new role pretending I'm an expert.

It's to arrive with a foundation I can build on.

And once I start, I can keep adding notes, asking questions, and improving the site as I learn.

New job. New challenges. Plenty to learn.

And a teacher who doesn't mind seventeen follow-up questions.

**WHICH IS PROBABLY JUST AS WELL.**

🤣
