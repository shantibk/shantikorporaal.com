### Mission Accomplished. Wrong Mission.

Last time we checked in on Achilles, he'd become an unstoppable project-finishing machine.

He'd fought the Trojans.

He'd fought a river.

**HE'D FOUGHT THE ENVIRONMENT ITSELF.**

🤣

And now?

He's finally found Hector.

The man who killed Patroclus.

The man Achilles is determined to destroy.

There's just one problem.

Hector isn't particularly enthusiastic about this meeting.

😂

He runs.

Achilles chases him around the walls of Troy.

Three times.

**THREE LAPS OF THE ENTIRE CITY.**

At this point, I'm not sure whether we're watching a battle or an extremely hostile team-building exercise.

🤣

Eventually, Athena intervenes.

She disguises herself as Hector's brother and tricks him into believing he has backup.

Hector stops running.

Turns to fight.

And discovers his support team has mysteriously disappeared.

**STAKEHOLDER SUPPORT: WITHDRAWN WITHOUT NOTICE.**

😂

Achilles kills Hector.

His revenge is complete.

**MISSION ACCOMPLISHED.**

Except...

Troy is still standing.

Helen hasn't been recovered.

And Patroclus is still dead.

So what exactly has Achilles achieved?

Well, he's eliminated Troy's greatest defender.

That's certainly progress for the Greek army.

But it wasn't the reason he was fighting anymore.

His personal objective had overtaken the original mission.

And here's where things get interesting.

Achilles hasn't lost his ability.

He's focused, determined, and delivering results.

But his grief has narrowed his world until revenge is the only objective he can see.

And even after killing Hector, his grief remains.

I think there's something very recognisable about that.

Not necessarily the revenge part.

Hopefully.

😂

But how easily our emotions can convince us that solving one particular problem will somehow fix everything.

We become so focused on getting an outcome, proving a point, or fixing something that's upset us that we stop checking whether we're still working towards the right goal.

**IS THIS STILL THE PROBLEM WE'RE SUPPOSED TO BE SOLVING?**

Because there's a difference between making progress and making progress towards the thing that actually matters.

Achilles has achieved something significant.

But it hasn't resolved what drove him there.

His grief is still there.

His anger is still there.

Meanwhile, the Greeks still have a war to finish.

The project had made progress.

**An objective has been achieved.**

Just not the objective anyone originally signed up for.

**AND THE PROJECT SPONSOR WOULD LIKE TO KNOW WHY TROY IS STILL STANDING.**

🤣
