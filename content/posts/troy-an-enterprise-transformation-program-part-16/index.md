### He's Back. And He's Everyone's Problem.

Achilles is back.

And he's got ENERGY.

🤣

Remember, while everyone else has been fighting this war, Achilles has spent a considerable amount of time sitting out the project.

The rest of the team?

Exhausted.

Overworked.

Probably questioning every career decision that brought them here.

And then Achilles arrives.

**GOOD MORNING, TEAM! READY TO SMASH SOME GOALS?!**

😂

He's that colleague who comes back from annual leave at 8am, full of enthusiasm, before you've even had your first coffee.

Except Achilles isn't here to improve team morale.

He's here for revenge.

And he's VERY good at his job.

With his new armour, he charges back into battle and starts cutting through the Trojan forces.

He's so effective that Zeus allows the gods to intervene because Achilles might destroy Troy before its scheduled time.

**THE PROJECT IS NOW AT RISK OF EARLY DELIVERY.**

🤣

Achilles encounters Aeneas.

Poseidon has to rescue him.

He goes after Hector.

Apollo intervenes.

And then...

**THE RIVER GETS INVOLVED.**

😂

Achilles has killed so many Trojan soldiers that their bodies are clogging the River Scamander.

The river god tells him to stop.

Achilles is not particularly receptive to feedback.

So the river attacks him.

**HE'S NOW FIGHTING THE ENVIRONMENT.**

🤣

And honestly?

I think we've all been there.

Not literally fighting a river.

But so consumed by a deadline, a problem, or sheer determination that we start believing some very questionable things.

I don't need eight hours of sleep.

I can do five people's jobs.

Documentation?

**I'LL REMEMBER WHAT HAPPENED.**

😂

Suddenly we're a one-person project-finishing machine.

Everything is urgent.

Everything is achievable.

And anyone suggesting we slow down clearly doesn't understand the mission.

Even the laws of nature are starting to feel like unnecessary bureaucracy.

**WHY DOES THE HUMAN BODY REQUIRE MAINTENANCE WINDOWS?!**

🤣

And for a while, it can look incredibly effective.

Things get done.

Problems get solved.

Progress happens.

But it's not sustainable.

And eventually, the fog clears.

You're exhausted.

The project has moved forward.

And nobody, including you, is entirely sure how you got there.

Then you open your notes.

**WHAT ON EARTH DOES THAT MEAN?!**

😂

I've been there.

I've looked back at my own notes after one of those intense periods and wondered what language I was writing in.

And then, once the fog has cleared, I'm left exhausted, spending even more energy trying to reconstruct the decisions, timelines, and what actually happened.

**APPARENTLY, PAST ME DIDN'T BELIEVE IN HANDOVER NOTES.**

😂

Because projects don't just need progress.

They need documentation, handovers, and decisions people can understand afterwards.

That's the part we sometimes forget when we're celebrating the person who can seemingly do everything.

Achilles is still an extraordinary warrior.

He's still getting results.

But he's so consumed by grief and revenge that even a river has become an obstacle to overcome.

And that's the danger.

**BEING UNSTOPPABLE ISN'T ALWAYS THE SAME AS BEING EFFECTIVE.**

Especially when the cost of all that momentum only becomes visible afterwards.

So perhaps the question isn't whether Achilles is still a hero.

He is.

The question is whether anyone can slow him down before his extraordinary ability creates even bigger problems.

Because sometimes the most important thing a project needs isn't another heroic sprint.

**IT'S SOMEONE WILLING TO SAY, "MATE, GO HOME AND GET SOME SLEEP."**

🤣
