---
title: "The Unexpected Joy of Being a Cog in the Machine"
date: 2026-11-19T10:30:00+11:00
---
I went on holiday to Japan.

Without my laptop.

**WITHOUT. MY. LAPTOP.**

🤣

For most people, this probably doesn't qualify as a major life achievement.

But after years of running businesses?

Practically a spiritual experience.

😂

When you own a business, especially in the early days, you don't really go on holidays.

You work from a different location.

An ocean view is lovely.

But you're still answering emails, looking after clients, chasing contracts and worrying about payroll.

And if something goes spectacularly wrong?

**CONGRATULATIONS! YOU'RE MANAGEMENT.**

🤣

Then I became an employee.

Suddenly there were procedures, approvals and other people making the final decisions.

**A CONCERNING DEVELOPMENT.**

😂

But there was an unexpected perk.

I could finish work.

And actually finish work.

For my first two years as an employee, I worked my nine-to-five and left it there.

No side businesses. No new ventures.

Then I went to Japan.

Leaving my laptop behind felt strange.

But mostly, I loved it.

No clients to check on. No payroll to worry about.

Just a holiday.

The world carried on without me.

**RUDE, REALLY.**

🤣

Eventually, though, I missed creating things.

The ideas. The problem-solving. The excitement of building something and sharing it.

So I brought back side projects.

Enter Recruiter Bot: AI, workflows, career archaeology and a digital development team that keeps assigning me homework.

🤣

The difference?

**It doesn't have to make money.**

I can build something because I'm curious, because I want to learn, or simply because it's fun.

No customers required.

And that's helped me realise something I didn't expect.

**I think I actually prefer being an employee.**

I still miss the independence of owning a business, choosing my hours and building a business of my own.

But what I loved most was creating, building and getting people excited about an idea.

Managing the finances, operations and endless responsibility?

Not so much.

If I ever start another business, I'm finding a business or finance operations partner who enjoys that side of things.

I'll happily be the person building and promoting what we create.

For now, I'm enjoying being a cog in the machine.

It keeps running when I clock off.

And my creativity gets to be mine again.

**NO BUSINESS PLAN REQUIRED.**

🤣
