Let me take you back to 19-year-old me.

I had a steady full-time job.

I'd saved enough for a small deposit on a unit.

With help from my parents and the First Home Owner Grant, I was on track to buy my first home.

**A VERY SENSIBLE PLAN.**

Except...

I had another idea.

🤣

I'd been doing bookkeeping and wanted to turn it into my own full-time business.

My own clients.

My own hours.

My own boss.

The idea was exciting!

It was also absolutely terrifying.

What if nobody wanted to work with me?

What if people didn't like how I trained them?

What if nobody trusted a 19-year-old to manage their books?

**WHAT IF I WAS MAKING A TERRIBLE MISTAKE?**

😂

So naturally...

I quit my steady job.

Took my house deposit.

Bought myself a computer and a laptop.

Signed up as an MYOB Partner.

Built a very basic website.

And YBotha Pty Ltd was born.

**GOODBYE, HOUSE DEPOSIT. HELLO, BUSINESS EXPENSES.**

🤣

I started with some clients through my parents' management consultancy and contacts of my own.

A couple of months later, I was working six and a half days a week just to keep up.

Turns out, being your own boss doesn't necessarily mean working fewer hours.

**WHO KNEW?**

😂

That was 18 years ago.

Since then, I've started a marriage, rebuilt my life after it ended, and stepped into a 9–5 job at 35 after spending 15 years running my own businesses.

And somewhere in between, I've started (and finished) more projects than I can count.

Some worked.

Some didn't.

Some turned into things I never could have imagined.

But here's something that hasn't changed.

**I STILL GET THE JITTERS WHEN I START SOMETHING NEW.**

Every. Single. Time.

Will I be good at this?

Will I know enough?

What if I get it wrong?

But alongside all those questions is something much louder.

**OOOH, WHAT AM I GOING TO LEARN?!**

🤣

Because my curiosity has always been bigger than my fear.

And I actually love those nervous, excited, slightly terrifying feelings.

They make me feel alive.

They remind me that I care.

That I'm stepping into something unfamiliar, with a whole new world of things to discover.

And today, I'm starting another new chapter.

I'm excited.

I'm nervous.

And yes, I'm still wondering whether I can really do this next step.

But I think 19-year-old me would recognise that feeling.

She'd probably be delighted to know I haven't lost it.

**HERE WE GO AGAIN!**

🤣
